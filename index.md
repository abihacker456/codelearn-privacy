# CodeLearn Pro — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 10/1/2026**

CodeLearn Pro does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

## What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. It does not include analytics, ads, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally on your device only:

- Which lessons you have completed
- Which lessons you have bookmarked
- Your theme preference (light or dark)
- Your recent search terms

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Code execution

When you tap "Run Code," the code you typed is sent over HTTPS to the public Judge0 Community Edition API for execution. The response is returned to your device. Judge0 does not require an account and does not associate the code with any user identity. See https://ce.judge0.com for details.
